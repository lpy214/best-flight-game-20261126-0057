**Ridgeline Courier.** An original chase-view flight game that plays offline in a desktop browser. You fly the Kestrel K-4, a red-and-cream bush plane, over Greywater Valley — patchwork fields, a winding river, a lake, forested hills, a small town and a mountain wall on both sides. Three sorties: a calm morning ring run, a timed mail delivery at golden hour, and a night storm pass with gusting crosswinds. Throttle, bank and pitch trade against each other — fly too slow and the nose drops, fly too hot and the fuel gauge falls faster than you would like — and every sortie ends the way a bush flight should: a landing on runway 36, wings level and slow, or not at all. Keyboard and mouse, title screen to debrief, with synthesised engine, wind, ring and crash sounds and a lit cockpit: compass strip, airspeed and altitude tapes, artificial horizon, throttle, fuel and damage gauges, and a guidance arrow to the next ring.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser and click a sortie. No build step, no network — the game is plain static files under `game/`.

## Controls

- **Fly:** mouse position, or ← → ↑ ↓ / WASD
- **Throttle:** mouse wheel, or Shift / Ctrl
- **Airbrake:** Space
- **Pause:** P · **Restart sortie:** R · **Menu:** Esc · **Sound:** M
